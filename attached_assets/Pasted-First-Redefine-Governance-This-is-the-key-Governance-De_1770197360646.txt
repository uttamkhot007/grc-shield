First: Redefine “Governance” (This is the key)

Governance = Decision intelligence + accountability + guardrails across the enterprise

A world-class governance module must:

Be authoritative (single source of truth)

Be connected (feeds Risk, Compliance, Privacy, BCM, ITSM)

Be dynamic (changes propagate automatically)

Be AI-assisted (humans approve, AI accelerates)
